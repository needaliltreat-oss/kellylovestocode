About

I've been building for the web for more than twenty years, from a design studio in London to a bank in Charlotte to my own music platform in New York. Today I work as a fractional CTO: I come in, make the technical calls and build what needs building.

Most recently I co-founded GigBag and built it as CTO and sole developer, taking four products from zero to closed beta. Before that I ran Take Me To Your Pixel, my own web design studio, where I took clients from a napkin sketch to a launched site, and I led a platform rebuild as UX architect for Carolinas AGC. My first leadership role was VP of Technology at Step Up 4 Learning, where I ran the platform and the development team behind an online writing program.

I speak "geek" and I speak business, so I can sit between your developers and every level of management and make sure both sides are heard.
